# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project welcomes constructive participation around reproducible forecasting, inventory simulation, and decision-system evaluation. The standard for discussion is simple: be respectful, specific, evidence-based, and focused on improving the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat other people with respect, including when disagreeing.
- Keep feedback technical, actionable, and tied to observable behavior or documentation.
- Use inclusive language and avoid personal attacks.
- Be clear about assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty.
- Respect privacy and do not post credentials, private datasets, or personal information.
- Report security-sensitive concerns privately rather than opening a public issue.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Posting secrets, credentials, or confidential data.
- Disrupting issues, pull requests, reviews, or discussions with off-topic or hostile comments.
- Misrepresenting results, evidence, authorship, or affiliation.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns by email: dfr@esmad.ipp.pt.

Include the relevant links, screenshots, dates, and a short description of what happened. Reports will be reviewed with attention to privacy, context, and proportional response.

## Enforcement

Possible responses include clarification, editing or removal of content, temporary restriction from project spaces, or permanent restriction from project spaces. Serious safety, privacy, or security issues may be escalated to the relevant platform provider.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, and documentation related to this repository.
