# Business Parameter Registry

## Purpose

Economic parameters turn abstract overage and underage costs into owned, dated, auditable business assumptions. No universal cost ratio is hard-coded.

## Required Metadata

Each parameter records name, value, confidence range, unit, currency, owner, source, effective dates, retailer, category, and store type where applicable.

## Parameter Families

- Gross margin and lost margin.
- Substitution and delayed-purchase probability.
- Disposal, markdown, handling, replenishment, holding, and working-capital costs.
- Customer-service or contractual floors.
- Staff effort, override cost, and order-volatility cost.
- Carbon or waste externality reported separately from financial cost.

## Workflow

Stakeholders should sign off parameter sets by retailer, category, or store type. Sensitivity reports should show whether the selected policy remains stable across plausible parameter ranges before operational recommendation.
