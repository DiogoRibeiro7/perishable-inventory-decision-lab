# Retail Adaptation 30/60/90 Plan

## First 30 days

- Map sales, inventory, waste, delivery, product, price, promotion, and calendar sources into the daily contract.
- Reconcile product identifiers and supplier mappings with effective dates.
- Define service, waste, and cost metrics with operations and finance stakeholders.
- Run historical backtests with strict decision-time cutoffs.
- Identify stockout-censored demand and unreliable inventory segments.

## First 60 days

- Add segment-level forecast calibration and policy evaluation.
- Compare policy candidates under agreed service floors and cost assumptions.
- Build monitoring thresholds for data freshness, forecast calibration, order validity, and policy drift.
- Dry-run publication against a non-operational table.
- Document rollback and ownership for each batch step.

## First 90 days

- Pilot a narrow category or region with manual review before execution.
- Track availability, waste, substitutions, overrides, and store feedback.
- Review cost assumptions and service constraints against observed operations.
- Expand only after publication checks, monitoring, and support playbooks work during live incidents.
