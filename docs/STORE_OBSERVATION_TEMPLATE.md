## Workflow Checklist

- Order cutoff.
- Delivery window.
- Shelf filling.
- Back-room stock.
- Waste recording.
- Stock count.
- Promotion or display.
- Substitution.
- Case pack or storage.
- Local event.
- Staff override.
- Escalation.
